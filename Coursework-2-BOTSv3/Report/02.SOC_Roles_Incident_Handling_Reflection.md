# SOC Roles and Incident Handling Reflection

SOC Analyst (Tier 1): Anything detected by the security solution would pass through these analysts first. These are the first responders to any detection. SOC Tier 1 Analysts perform basic alert triage to determine if a specific detection is harmful. They also report these detections through proper channels.
SOC Analyst (Tier 2): While Tier 1 does the first-level analysis, some detections may require deeper investigation. Tier 2 Analysts help them dive deeper into the investigations and correlate the data from multiple data sources to perform a proper analysis.
SOC Analyst (Tier 3): Tier 3 Analysts are experienced professionals who proactively look for any threat indicators and support in the incident response activities. The critical severity detection reported by Tier 1 and Tier 2 Analysts are often security incidents that need detailed responses, including containment, eradication, and recovery. This is where tier 3 analysts’ experience comes in handy.

Throughout this coursework, I have been acting as a Tier 2 SOC analyst, as opposed to Tier 1 which analyses that something *has* happened, while tier 2 explains *how* it has happened. By doing this coursework we have identified how the Frothly company has been involved by a security incident, and acting as a Tier 3 analyst by providing "lessons learned" and "recommendations" for the company going forward to avoid another incident of a similar nature.
